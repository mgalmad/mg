# Behavioral

## Story bank (help the candidate build 8–10 stories, each reusable across themes)
For each story, capture: **Context** (1–2 lines, with numbers) → **Your decision
point** → **Actions you personally took** → **Result** (quantified) →
**What you'd do differently** → **Which themes it covers**.

Themes to cover at least twice across the bank:
- Delivering under ambiguity or a hard deadline
- Disagreement with a manager or peer (and when you were wrong)
- A failure or incident you owned
- Influencing without authority / cross-team alignment
- Raising the bar: mentoring, hiring, quality
- Trade-off between speed and quality (or tech debt)
- Changing direction based on data
- Staff+: setting technical direction; killing a project; an org-level mechanism you created

## Drilling technique
Ask the open question, then drill at least 3 layers deep:
1. "What specifically did *you* do?" (separates "we" from "I")
2. "What alternatives did you reject, and why?"
3. "What did the other person believe, and were they partly right?"
4. "How do you know it worked? What was the number?"
5. "What would have happened without you?"

## Red flags to note
- Only "we", with no individual decisions
- Blaming others; no lessons learned
- Results without numbers, or numbers without a baseline
- Scope well below the target level
- Rehearsed polish that collapses at the second follow-up
