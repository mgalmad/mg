# Crypto and Swiss tax (not tax advice; verify each tax year with your canton)

- **Private capital gains** on crypto are tax-free for private individuals. **Capital losses aren't deductible.**
- **Professional trader risk:** the same ESTV Kreisschreiben Nr. 36 safe harbour applies as for securities. That means holding for at least 6 months, yearly volume no more than 5× the portfolio, gains under 50% of income, no leverage, and derivatives only for hedging. **A bot that trades every 4 hours can fail the holding-period and volume tests on its own.** If you're classified as professional, gains become income, subject to income tax and AHV. Track turnover and median holding period every week (`self-update.md`).
- **Wealth tax:** crypto holdings at 31 December are declared at the ESTV year-end rates (the Kursliste).
- **Income:** staking, lending, airdrops and mining count as taxable income at market value on receipt.
- **Documentation:** the hash-chained ledger and exchange CSV exports form your audit trail. Keep them for 10 years.
- The agiprolabs tax skills (`cost-basis-engine`, `crypto-tax-export`) are useful for **cost-basis bookkeeping and exports**. Their US-specific outputs (wash sales, Form 8949) don't apply to a Swiss resident.

Sources: ESTV KS 36 (see `sources.json`). Cantonal practice varies, so confirm with your cantonal tax office.
